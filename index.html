Chronicle I • World Lore

The Sundering of the Spires

Whether this is your first step onto Vana'diel's soil or a return to a beloved homeland, the world has changed—and ancient echoes now answer your call.

01 • The Cataclysm

When the Ancient Crags Fractured

Across Vana'diel, the five ancient Crags—Holla, Dem, Mea, Vahzl, and Al'Taieu—were built to anchor the Mothercrystals and safeguard the flow of life energy. During a silent, celestial tremor known as the Sundering, their upper conduits fractured into towering, resonant monoliths of raw aether: the Soulspires.

02 • The Sixty-Six Shards

Memories of Forgotten Champions

When the Spires shattered, they did not release empty magic—they unleashed the crystallized memories, combat arts, and unbroken wills of sixty-six forgotten champions from Vana'diel's lost ages. Raining across the continents, these shards hardened into Soul Crystals, offering three distinct paths attuned to each of the twenty-two classic disciplines of war and magic.

03 • The Adventurer's Calling

Awaken What Lies Dormant

To socket a Soul Crystal is to invite a forgotten champion's spirit into your own—opening new ways to wield steel, weave spells, and stand beside your allies. As the realm's creatures grow fiercer in the wake of the aether surge, adventurers must seek out these shards and master their dormant arts before darkness consumes the land.

First-Time Adventurer

New to Final Fantasy XI?

Think of Soulspire XI as a classic, deeply rewarding cooperative fantasy RPG modernized for today. You don't need a twenty-year wiki degree to enjoy it—just pick a job that excites you, step into a vast living world, and learn naturally as you adventure.

  • Simple One-Click Launcher: No confusing legacy setup—download, patch, and play without the hassle.
  • Clear In-Game Guidance: Streamlined onboarding and intuitive modern menus help you find your footing quickly.
  • Welcoming Community: A friendly Discord filled with fellow adventurers happy to answer questions and group up.
Seasoned Hero

Returning Veteran of Vana'diel?

All twenty-two iconic jobs and the deliberate, tactical soul of Vana'diel are right where your heart left them—now reimagined with genuine discovery. Familiar zones hold new mysteries, and beloved jobs open into uncharted archetypes.
